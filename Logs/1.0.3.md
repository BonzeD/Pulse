# What’s New in Pulse 1.0.3

Build a personal music cosmos in Atlas, enjoy dimensional lyrics, and diagnose playback with clearer tools.

## New

- **Atlas — your personal music cosmos.** Look around a projected celestial sphere, open settings over the sky, and add a track from its menu into open space or an existing constellation. The sidebar playlists of the active service are selectable; their tracks are stored as a snapshot. Add saved playlists or hand-picked tracks as stars. Connect stars into named constellations and arrange, rotate, move and edit their links. A wave and camera transition opens a star; playback makes the source star red and its constellation pulse softly. The animation is visual, not beat detection.

- **A lyric drum for the sidebar.** Lines curve away above and below the current lyric, keeping the singing line front and center. Manual scrolling and word-by-word highlighting still work. Turn it off in Settings → Lyrics → Scrolling; reduced-motion uses a flat list.
- **More 3D shelf choices.** Choose Off, Side or Stage for the Mineradio queue shelf directly in Pulse.

- **Audio diagnostics in the Developer window.** Record a playback session, then copy or save a log to help investigate slow loading or failed tracks. Recording is off by default. Audio logs leave out track names, links, account credentials and file paths.

## Improvements

- **YouTube starts first.** Online tracks now try the built-in YouTube player before the backup audio loader. Songs that work there no longer have to wait for a separate audio link to be prepared.
- **Automatic recovery when a playback method fails.** If the built-in player reports an error or cannot start, Pulse tries the backup route while keeping your playback position and pause state. Startup time still depends on YouTube and your connection.
- **Less unnecessary background loading.** Hovering over songs and preparing the next queue item no longer starts separate audio-link searches. Whole-track audio analysis is not started while the built-in YouTube player is being used.
- **Smoother changes between player views.** Switching between the cover, video and visualizer views keeps the current built-in player running instead of restarting the song.
- **Local music stays local.** Downloaded and imported files continue to use the file player without needing YouTube or an internet connection. Audius keeps its own playback route.
- **Lighter background operation.** Hidden visualizers stop drawing, and unused visual resources and temporary caches are released after a short delay. Hidden mini-players stop refreshing and restore the current track and lyrics when reopened.
- **Faster reuse of matched recordings.** Apple Music and Spotify tracks can reuse a saved recording match without fetching the same catalog details again.

## Fixes

### Playback and controls

- **Restored the earlier embedded-first audio behavior.** Later experimental playback timers and background recovery checks have been removed after reports of silent or slow playback. The built-in YouTube player still starts first, with backup playback if it fails.

- **Fixed Mineradio queue cards missing changes** when songs in the middle were reordered or renamed.
- **Fixed repeated clicks on a loading Mineradio song** accidentally pausing the pending selection.
- **Kept Mineradio console choices in sync with Pulse.** Preset, quality, shelf mode and lyric visibility no longer revert on an unrelated settings change.
- **Restored available spectrum data for visual effects that request floating-point samples.** This uses the existing captured signal, without creating another audio output.
- **Kept the selected lyrics panel accessible in narrow windows**, with a two-row header at small widths so navigation, search and account controls have their own space.

- **More reliable track switching and seeking.** Old requests cannot take over a newer selection, and your chosen position is preserved while a track loads or changes playback route.
- **Better handling of rejected or expired audio links.** Pulse checks that a link serves media and can refresh a rejected backup link before reporting failure. Canceled streams stop downloading, and paused tracks are not skipped automatically.
- **More reliable DJ loading.** Late requests no longer replace a newer deck selection. Failed loads clear their busy state, and a failed transition restores the original deck’s volume.

### Security

- **Updated the app’s browser engine.** This build includes an Electron update with certificate-security fixes, without changing the Pulse version or resetting your profile.

### Accounts, lyrics and diagnostics

- **Saved YouTube sessions are restored at startup.** Temporary verification failures no longer erase saved credentials. Choosing Sign out still keeps you signed out.
- **Clearer sign-in status.** A saved account name is not shown as a confirmed session when verification fails. Compact warnings keep account controls usable in narrow windows.
- **Fixed switching back to Spicy Lyrics.** Your selected provider is applied consistently in Settings and the lyrics menu. Selecting it again retries the request, without changing custom lyrics or synchronization.
- **Stopped older lyrics requests from replacing newer results** after quickly changing providers.
- **More useful audio logs.** Logs distinguish playback from analysis, identify failed loading stages and record safe error categories without including raw error text or account details.
- **Fixed copying larger audio logs.** Audio-log copying no longer uses the smaller limit intended for ordinary developer reports.

## Good to Know

- This release repairs the existing Mineradio integration; it does not include every feature of the current upstream app. The separate Folia interface and its extra lyric renderers are not included.

- Some YouTube videos do not allow embedded playback. Backup playback may also be unavailable because of service restrictions, sign-in requirements or rejected media links. Pulse cannot guarantee access to every recording.
- Built-in YouTube audio does not pass through Pulse’s normal audio decks. EQ, DJ mixing and effects from that audio chain do not apply to it; whole-track AutoSync analysis is also not run for that playback route. Local/downloaded media and direct audio keep their existing processing paths.
- Spicy Lyrics may credit Apple Music or another original source with “via Spicy Lyrics.” This still means the lyrics came through Spicy Lyrics. A missing song may use a clearly labeled fallback.
- Audio logs omit sensitive identifiers, but screenshots and Chromium DevTools can show page content. Review them before sharing.
- Atlas starts empty. Its map, positions and constellations are stored on this device. Playlist stars follow the saved library playlist while it exists; a copy is kept as a fallback. Custom selection shows up to 1,000 library tracks. Each star can belong to one constellation, and edits cannot leave a constellation disconnected.
- Atlas uses the existing playback queue and does not upload the map. Local and downloaded media use the existing file player. Online tracks may need a connection. Atlas is available in Russian and English, with English fallback for other languages.
- Version remains **1.0.3**. This delivery includes **Windows x64 setup and portable builds**, without a digital signature. Installing or updating is designed to keep your profile; uninstall has separate data-removal choices. Native installation, sound, background switching and real music-service availability still need checking on the target computer.
