# What’s New in Pulse 1.0.2

## New

- **Audius is now available.** Search and play tracks, explore artists and playlists—all without signing in. Likes and playlists are saved locally in Pulse. Audius downloads aren’t supported yet.
- **First-launch setup, redesigned for Pulse.** Choose your language, music service, graphics preferences and app behavior in a new interface that follows your Pulse theme, with smooth transitions and clear step-by-step progress. Skip it or run it again from Settings without losing your library.
- **A redesigned startup animation** featuring prismatic glass and shifting colors.
- **Prism—a full-screen, music-reactive visualizer.** Quickly click the Pulse logo five times to open it. The Pulse lettering takes its colors from the current track’s artwork, and a miniature spectrum now replaces the caption beneath it. Press Esc or double-click to exit.
- **A dedicated developer window.** Quickly right-click the Pulse logo five times to open tools in a separate window pinned above Pulse. Access Chromium DevTools, pick UI elements, check layout boundaries, record frame intervals, inspect audio and app state, and copy a screenshot or diagnostic report.

## Improvements & Fixes

- Moved the current track’s audio source controls from above the player to **Settings → Client → Audius**. Recording selection, original links and match reset are still available there.
- Made panels smoother to open and close.
- Fixed clipped menus and cramped tabs in narrow windows.
- Fixed unsaved lyrics edits being lost when switching editor tabs.
- Removed harsh lighting seams and reduced shimmering on rocks in the **Black Sea** scene.
- Reduced unnecessary work in visualizers and fixed cases where they stayed hidden after switching modes.
- Developer layout overlays are temporary and never saved to your preferences. Diagnostic reports omit track names, account details and file paths. Screenshots and Chromium DevTools may expose page content—review before sharing.
- Added a **Logs** folder with readable release notes.
